**UI/UX guidelines**

The application should be minimal and not loaded with things to see, each thing saprated by cards and elements and limited information on the first look.
    
Quantum Care should feel like a **modern healthcare platform rather than a traditional hospital portal**.

The interface must combine:

* Minimalism
* Glassmorphism
* Soft blur effects
* Material Design principles
* Clear typography
* Large and accessible controls
* Gentle animations
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Calm healthcare aesthetics
* Consistent spacing
* Responsive layouts
The application has two visual themes.

* Light Pink
